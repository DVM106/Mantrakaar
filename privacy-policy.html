    Privacy Policy

    Effective Date: 1 February 2026 Last Revised: February 2026

    This Privacy Policy describes how Mantrakaar Digital LLP ("Mantrakaar", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you visit our website or engage our digital services. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please discontinue use of our website.

    Information We Collect

    We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you submit an inquiry form, contact us by email, subscribe to our newsletter, or request a consultation. This may include:

    • Full name and business name
    • Email address and phone number
    • Business requirements and project details shared in correspondence
    • Technical metadata such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited (collected automatically via analytics)

    We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as financial account numbers or government-issued identification) through this website.

    How We Use Your Information

    Information collected is used strictly for the following purposes:

    • To respond to project inquiries and service requests
    • To deliver agreed digital services and project deliverables
    • To send project updates, billing correspondence, and service notifications
    • To send marketing communications where you have explicitly opted in
    • To improve the performance and usability of our website through anonymised analytics

    We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

    Cookies and Tracking Technologies

    Our website uses essential cookies to store your preferences (including dark mode and language settings) across sessions. We may also use third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) that collect anonymised usage data. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some site functionality may be affected.

    Data Retention

    We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Client project data is retained for a minimum of three (3) years following project completion for record-keeping and warranty purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

    Data Security

    Mantrakaar implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), access control policies, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    Third-Party Services

    Our website may contain links to external websites or may utilise third-party platforms (such as payment processors, hosting providers, or CRM tools) in the course of delivering services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

    Your Rights

    Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

    • Access the personal data we hold about you
    • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
    • Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
    • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time
    • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

    To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at parag@mantrakaar.com.

    Changes to This Policy

    We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of our website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
